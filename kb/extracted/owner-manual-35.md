# Wire Weld Diagnosis – Workpiece Heat Control / Weld Penetration

### FIGURE: penetration-diagnosis-cross-sections
**Caption:** none (section header serves as implicit caption: "Wire Weld Diagnosis – Workpiece Heat Control / Weld Penetration")
**Type:** diagram
**Description:** Three side-by-side cross-section diagrams of a weld joint on a gray workpiece, each showing a different bead penetration profile, arranged left to right along a heat spectrum:
1. Left diagram labeled "INADEQUATE PENETRATION" — shows a shallow, rounded weld bead sitting on top of the joint with a visible gap/crack running down into the joint (the two workpiece pieces are not fused deep into the base metal). Labeled below as "Not hot enough."
2. Middle diagram labeled "PROPER PENETRATION" — shows a weld bead fully fused into the joint with a smooth, deep, evenly-tapered penetration profile reaching into the joint without excess. Labeled below as "Ideal heat."
3. Right diagram labeled "EXCESS PENETRATION OR BURN-THROUGH" — shows a weld bead with penetration extending completely through the workpiece thickness (bulging through the back side), indicating melt-through. Labeled below as "Too hot."
Below the three diagrams is a horizontal double-headed arrow bar spanning the full width, divided into three text zones: "Not hot enough" (left), "Ideal heat" (center), "Too hot" (right), visually representing a heat/temperature continuum.
Below the arrow, two columns of corrective instructions:
- Left column, "How to increase workpiece heat and increase penetration: (to weld THICKER workpieces properly)":
  a. Increase weld current
  b. Decrease travel speed
  c. Use faster wire feed
  d. Use shorter CTWD
- Right column, "How to reduce workpiece heat and limit penetration: (to weld THINNER workpieces properly)":
  e. Decrease weld current
  f. Increase travel speed
  g. Use slower wire feed
  h. Use longer CTWD

## Example Wire Weld Diagrams

### FIGURE: example-wire-weld-diagrams
**Caption:** Example Wire Weld Diagrams
**Type:** diagram
**Description:** A row of six illustrated weld bead examples, each consisting of (top) a cross-section end view of the bead on the workpiece, (middle) a long side-view illustration of the bead's surface ripple/texture pattern, and (bottom) a text label naming the defect/condition, a "TO CORRECT:" instruction, and in the last three panels a small torch/gun icon with an arrow illustrating travel direction or CTWD angle. The six panels, left to right, are:
1. **Good Weld** — Cross-section: smooth, evenly rounded bead. Side view: uniform, tightly-spaced ripple pattern. Label: "Good Weld." No correction needed (no "TO CORRECT" text shown for this panel).
2. **Voltage Too Low or Wire Feed Too Slow** — Cross-section: small, peaked/humped bead sitting high on the surface. Side view: tall, narrow, rope-like ripple pattern with a rounded blob at the end. Label: "Voltage Too Low or Wire Feed Too Slow." TO CORRECT: "Increase Output Voltage or Increase Wire Feed Speed."
3. **Voltage Too High or Wire Feed Too Fast** — Cross-section: wide, flat, spread-out bead. Side view: wide, splattered/spattered ripple pattern with scattered spatter dots around the bead. Label: "Voltage Too High or Wire Feed Too Fast." TO CORRECT: "Decrease Output Voltage or Decrease Wire Feed Speed."
4. **Travel Speed Too Fast** — Cross-section: small, narrow, elongated oval bead. Side view: narrow, elongated rope-like ripple pattern. Label: "Travel Speed Too Fast." TO CORRECT: (no text instruction, only icon) — torch/gun icon with arrow and text "travel slower."
5. **Travel Speed Too Slow** — Cross-section: wide, rounded, puddled bead. Side view: wide, smooth, blob-like puddled pattern (larger rounded mass). Label: "Travel Speed Too Slow." TO CORRECT: (no text instruction, only icon) — torch/gun icon with arrow and text "travel faster."
6. **CTWD Too Long or Wrong Polarity** — Cross-section: irregular bead with scattered spatter dots around it. Side view: irregular, spatter-covered ripple pattern with many scattered spatter dots. Label: "CTWD Too Long or Wrong Polarity." TO CORRECT: "Check Polarity and maintain less than 1/2" CTWD" — accompanied by a torch/gun icon with a downward arrow illustrating contact-tip-to-work distance and the text "1/2" CTWD" pointing to the arc/workpiece contact point.
Vertical divider lines separate each of the six panels.
