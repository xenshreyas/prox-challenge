# Controls

## Front Panel Controls

### FIGURE: front-panel-controls
**Caption:** none
**Type:** diagram
**Description:** A labeled front-view diagram of the Vulcan OmniPro 220 welder, showing the front panel with callout lines pointing to each control/feature. The unit displays "VULCAN" and "OMNIPRO 220" branding above an LCD screen. Callouts (no numbers used, only text labels with leader lines) are as follows, arranged roughly top-to-bottom, left-to-right:

- **Home Button** – small square button to the left of the LCD display, labeled "HOME" on the panel, used with a triangular/arrow icon below it.
- **Back Button** – small square button to the right of the LCD display, labeled "BACK" on the panel.
- **Control Knob** – the center knob below the LCD display (large circular knob with a downward-pointing triangle indicator), used for navigating/adjusting settings.
- **LCD Display** – the rectangular screen in the center of the control panel, between the Home and Back buttons.
- **Left Knob** – circular knob to the left of the Control Knob, marked with "+A" (plus/amperage) symbol.
- **Right Knob** – circular knob to the right of the Control Knob, marked with a "V" (voltage) symbol.
- **Power Switch** – rocker switch (I/O, on/off) located below the control panel, to the left side, next to the MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket.
- **Storage Compartment** – the vented/louvered compartment area to the right of the Power Switch, below the "VULCAN" logo band, used for storage.
- **MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket** – circular socket on the lower-left area of the front panel, used to connect the MIG gun or spool gun cable.
- **Spool Gun Gas Outlet** – circular threaded outlet at the bottom-left of the panel, used to connect the Spool Gun gas hose (per the note on this page).
- **Negative Socket** – circular threaded socket at the bottom of the panel, marked with a "−" (minus) symbol.
- **Wire Feed Power Cable** – small round socket at the bottom center of the panel, positioned between the Negative and Positive Sockets.
- **Positive Socket** – circular threaded socket at the bottom-right of the panel, marked with a "+" (plus) symbol.

The diagram illustrates the complete layout of the front control panel including all user-accessible sockets, switches, knobs, buttons, and the display, with straight leader lines connecting each label to its corresponding part on the welder.
