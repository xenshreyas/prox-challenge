# HOW TO CHOOSE A WELDER

**IMPORTANT!** IDENTIFY YOUR INPUT VOLTAGE: DO YOU HAVE 120 VOLT OR 240 VOLT?

This page is a decision chart comparing four welding processes — **Flux-Cored/FCAW**, **MIG/GMAW**, **Stick/SMAW**, and **TIG/GTAW** — across skill level, shielding gas needs, material type, material thickness, typical applications, and weld cleanliness. It also includes a "MIG or Flux Cored?" comparison table and a Duty Cycle explanation box.

## Duty Cycle Explanation Box

**The Duty Cycle** is the number of minutes, within a 10 minute period, a welding process can produce the specified welding current without overheating.

**DUTY CYCLE EXAMPLE**

165A @ 30% Duty Cycle
- 3 Minutes Welding
- 7 Minutes Resting
(illustrated with a clock/pie-chart icon showing the 3-minute/7-minute split)
