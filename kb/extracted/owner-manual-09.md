# Interior Controls

### FIGURE: interior-controls-diagram
**Caption:** none
**Type:** diagram
**Description:** A labeled line-drawing (front/interior view with the front panel or door open) of the Vulcan OmniPro 220 welder, showing the internal wire feed compartment and its controls, with leader lines pointing from each label to its corresponding part on the machine. The carrying handle and side handles are visible at the top. Callouts (no numbered legend; all are directly labeled with leader lines):
- **Cold Wire Feed Switch** — a small rectangular switch/button located at the top center of the interior compartment, above the wire feed mechanism.
- **Feed Tensioner** — points to the tensioning arm/lever mounted above the wire feed roller assembly on the right side of the compartment; used to adjust pressure on the wire feed rollers.
- **Idler Arm** — the pivoting arm on the wire feed roller assembly (right side) that swings open/closed to release or clamp the wire against the feed roller.
- **Wire Feed Mechanism** — the drive assembly (motor/gearbox with roller housing, labeled "VULCAN") mounted on the right-center of the compartment that pulls wire from the spool and pushes it through the liner.
- **Wire Spool** — the large ribbed knob/spool holder on the left side of the compartment where the wire spool mounts, labeled "VULCAN" on the knob face.
- **Spool Knob** — the central knob on the wire spool holder (left side) that secures/tensions the spool in place.
- **Wire Inlet Liner** — the wire guide tube/liner running from the wire spool area into the wire feed mechanism, guiding the wire into the rollers.
- **Feed Roller Knob** — a knob on the wire feed mechanism (right side) used to adjust or release the feed roller.
- **Foot Pedal Socket** — a round multi-pin connector socket at the bottom center of the interior compartment for connecting an optional foot pedal.
- **Wire Feed Control Socket** — a round multi-pin connector socket next to the Foot Pedal Socket (to its right) at the bottom of the interior compartment, for connecting a wire feed control device.
